Beginners Courses

For people new to archery

6 weeks tuition from AGB qualified instructors/coaches.

If you have never tried archery before or would like to get back into it, a beginners course is for you. It is an easy and fun way for beginners to get a complete introduction to the sport, in six fun lessons. Beginners courses are capped at 9 participants per course. All you need to do is wear comfortable clothing and bring a drink. All of the equipment will be provided for you.

Beginners Course Availability

We run beginners courses throughout the year, with the schedule being advertised around 6 weeks in advance. Check back often for up to date information on course availability and to book your place.

About our junior members.

Placeholder content about our junior members. We are now running regular junior archery coaching sessions for youngsters aged 7 to 13 looking to get into the sport. Much more than a 'have-a-go' session at a holiday park or school residential, you'll learn from experienced archers and coaches how to shoot safely and improve your technique. These short sessions are filled with fun and games. A parent or guardian should stay to supervise all juniors. ​The sessions run as short blocks, usually of 4 weeks, which must be booked and paid for in advance.

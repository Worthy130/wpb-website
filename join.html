THE BEGINNERS COURSE

Six weeks. A new skill for life.

Our Beginners Courses are made for people new to archery. You will learn the foundations of safe shooting from experienced coaches, using equipment supplied by the club.

What to expect

Six enjoyable Tuesday evening sessions that build your technique, safety awareness and confidence at your own pace.

What to bring

Comfortable clothing and a drink. Bows, arrows, arm guards and finger tabs are provided.

Who can join

Beginners Courses are usually suitable for people aged 14 and over. Under-18s must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.

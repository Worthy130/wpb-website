Your local archery club at Winnington Park, Northwich

Winnington Park Bowmen is one of Cheshire's longest-established archery clubs, welcoming beginners and experienced archers alike.

About Winnington Park Bowmen

Winnington Park Bowmen archery club was formed in Northwich, Cheshire, on the 26th July 1957. We're a friendly and inclusive club with two ranges, a 20 yard indoor range at Winnington Park Recreation Club, and a 100 yards/90 meter outdoor range at Moss Farm. The club is mainly focused on target archery but many members travel all over the country to take part in field and clout shoots as well as shoot at competitions throughout the year. Members shoot using a variety of bows including recurve, compound and longbow

How to join

In order to join our archery club for you must complete a beginners course. Once you have completed this course you will be invited to join us as a member should you wish to do so. There is sometimes a waiting list for beginners' courses but we'll try and get you started as soon as possible. Please check the joining page for more information. If you have already completed a course elsewhere we will meet you for an assessment to ensure you can shoot safely before joining.

What's the minimum age?

The ballpark answer for those wishing to complete a full beginners' course and join the club is 14 years old, however there have been exceptions made if we feel anyone younger is capable if following instruction and staying safe while shooting. Most younger archers should join us by attending our junior sessions. Please see the Juniors page for more information. All juniors under the age of 18 should be supervised by a parent of guardian at all times when attending a course or any sessions at the club. Short blocks of junior sessions are also available for younger archers. Regular attendance at these "juniorling" sessions will preprare young learners to become junior members once they have reached certain level of proficiency. Please see the Juniorlings page for more information.

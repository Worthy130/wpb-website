About Winnington Park Bowmen

Winnington Park Bowmen was formed in Northwich, Cheshire, on 26 July 1957.

We're a friendly and inclusive club with two shooting venues:

  • 20-yard indoor range at Winnington Park Recreation Club
  • 100-yard (90 metre) outdoor range at Moss Farm Leisure Complex

Our members shoot recurve, compound and longbow and regularly compete in target, field and clout archery throughout the UK.

How to join

To join Winnington Park Bowmen, you'll first need to complete one of our beginners' courses. Once you have completed this course you will be invited to join us as a member should you wish to do so. Our beginners' courses are very popular, so there may occasionally be a waiting list. We'll always aim to get you onto the next available course. Please check the joining page for more information. If you have already completed a course elsewhere we will meet you for an assessment to ensure you can shoot safely before joining.

What's the minimum age?

Our beginners' courses are generally suitable for people aged 14 and over.

In some circumstances we may accept younger archers if we feel they are able to follow instructions and shoot safely.

Children under 18 must always be accompanied by a parent or guardian.

Younger children can usually start with our Juniorlings sessions before progressing to full junior membership.

Short blocks of junior sessions are also available for younger archers. Regular attendance at these "juniorling" sessions will preprare young learners to become junior members once they have reached certain level of proficiency. Please see the Juniorlings page for more information.

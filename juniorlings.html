About our junior members who we affectionately call our Juniorlings.

Placeholder content about our junior members. WPB "Juniorlings" Winnington Park Bowmen “Juniorlings” are members who are still receiving close instruction on our Tuesday afternoon children’s sessions. Once they have reached a suitable level of proficiency and upon assessment by a WPB coach they may be granted membership but should continue to shoot only at Tuesday afternoon sessions and other specified special events under the close supervision of our coaches. If they continue to progress they will later be invited to join Tuesday evening coaching sessions, then other sessions alongside senior members.
